vgsales-analysis/

r · python

What drives video game sales? A statistical study of 16,598 games using non-parametric tests, bootstrap intervals and regression on heavily skewed data. Publisher size is the strongest factor (median 0.27M vs 0.07M units for major vs small publishers), and RPGs sell a median 42% of their units in Japan, against 0% for other genres. Classification models complement the analysis and confirm these factors describe groups, not individual games (R² = 0.11, 0.41 balanced accuracy).
